Privacy Policy

App: White Lion Boss (Android game)
Developer: Vatsal
Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains how the game "White Lion Boss" ("the app") handles information. The app is a simple offline runner game. We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or any other personal details ourselves.

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress (best score, coins, unlocked items, selected look and settings) only on your own device. This data is not sent to us. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage, this data is deleted.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads using Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and use certain information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and operating system information, app usage and approximate location. This is handled by Google under its own policies and not by us.

You can learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services here: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

3. Rewarded ads

Some features (for example reviving after a crash, doubling coins or claiming a bonus) are unlocked by watching an optional video ad. Watching these ads is always your choice.

4. We do not sell your data

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share any data with third parties except the advertising service described above.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data deletion

Because the data we keep is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. For data held by Google AdMob, please see Google's privacy controls.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page.
